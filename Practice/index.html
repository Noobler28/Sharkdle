How to practice

Practice with a randomly selected shark, relative, or mixed species at your own pace.

  • Choose Sharks, Relatives, or Mixed. There is no guess limit; keep guessing until you find the target.
  • Type a species name and choose it from the suggestions.
  • Each guess shows its Order, Family, Genus, Size, Depth, and Discovery Year. Mixed also shows whether your guess is a shark, ray, skate, or chimaera.
  • A green clue is an exact match; an uncolored clue differs. The year arrow points toward the target’s discovery year. Hover over Order, Family, or Genus for its common name when available.
  • Practice does not record your game stats or award XP. Find the target and choose Play Again to try another.
