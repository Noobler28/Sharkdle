What testers do

  • Preview updates: play new features and modes before release.

Tester benefits

Exclusive access

Fast feedback loop

Send bug reports and improvement ideas directly to the team. Your input shapes the final release.

Special rewards

How to get involved

Testers should:

  • Play the latest update daily.
  • Check for glitches in puzzles and resources.
  • Note anything that feels unfair or confusing.
  • Submit issues through the feedback channel.

What we’re looking for

  • Gameplay balance and scoring clarity.
  • UI layout issues on desktop and mobile.
  • Cosmetic unlock and progression accuracy.
  • Reliable cloud save and sync behavior.
