How to play

Take on a randomly selected shark, relative, or mixed species, then start another round whenever you’re ready.

  • Choose Sharks, Relatives, or Mixed. You get 8 guesses for Sharks or Relatives and 12 for Mixed.
  • Type a species name and choose it from the suggestions.
  • Each guess shows its Order, Family, Genus, Size, Depth, and Discovery Year. Mixed also shows whether your guess is a shark, ray, skate, or chimaera.
  • A green clue is an exact match; an uncolored clue differs. The year arrow points toward the target’s discovery year. Hover over Order, Family, or Genus for its common name when available.
  • If you are signed in and have enough pearls, you can reveal one undiscovered category per round for 25 pearls.
  • Wins can award XP and pearls. When signed in, wins and losses update your stats and streak. You can replay for another target at any time.
