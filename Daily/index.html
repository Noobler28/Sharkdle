How to play

Everyone gets the same target for each daily pool. Solve it before the day changes.

  • 🗂️Choose Sharks, Relatives, or Mixed. Sharks and Relatives give you 8 guesses; Mixed gives you 12.
  • 🔎Type a species name and choose it from the suggestions.
  • 📊Each guess shows its Order, Family, Genus, Size, Depth, and Discovery Year. Mixed also shows whether your guess is a shark, ray, skate, or chimaera.
  • 🟢A green clue is an exact match; an uncolored clue differs. The year arrow points toward the target’s discovery year. Hover over Order, Family, or Genus for its common name when available.
  • 💡If you are signed in and have enough pearls, you can reveal one undiscovered category per game for 25 pearls.
  • 📅Your daily result counts toward your Sharkdle stats. Sharks, Relatives, and Mixed each have their own target for the day.
