No Mercy Mode

The Rules

Six guesses. You get 20 seconds to name each shark; when time runs out, that guess is used. No Mercy is all about skill—only the elite can win.

  • Guess shark species by common or scientific name.
  • 🟢 Green means the category matches. 🟡 Yellow means it is close. 🔴 Red means it does not match. Only the category name and color are shown; the shark’s actual information stays hidden.
  • Categories are family, genus, size, depth, and year.
  • Previous guess clues disappear after your next guess.
  • Leaving the page or switching tabs ends the run. The Sharchive is unavailable during No Mercy.
  • No Mercy has its own stats; losses do not affect your normal streak. You can replay whenever you want.
  • Win and earn bragging rights. Send a screenshot of your win to the developer’s Discord DMs to receive a personal message from the developer.
