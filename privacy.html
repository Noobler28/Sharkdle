Sharkdle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Information We Collect

SharkdleBot may collect and store limited information needed to provide its features, including Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, shark sightings, Sharkdex progress, statistics, and server settings.

Messages

SharkdleBot may process Discord messages when required for interactive features such as shark encounters. General Discord conversations are not stored by SharkdleBot.

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate features such as Sharkdex progress, profiles, leaderboards, achievements, shark encounters, and server configuration.

Data Removal

Requests relating to stored SharkdleBot data can be made through the official Sharkdle Discord server.
