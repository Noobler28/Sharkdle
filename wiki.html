Welcome to the Sharkdle Wiki

I’m Connor Eagle, also known as Noobler28, the developer of Sharkdle. This is the reference for my game: how each mode works, what you can unlock, where rewards come from, and how to make sense of the clues and collections.

Sharkdle’s main challenge is to identify a mystery species from the information revealed by your guesses. There is also a Story campaign, SharkRNG, Shark Rescue Command, and a growing collection of achievements and profile cosmetics. Use this wiki when you want the details behind something you have found in the game.

What the different labels mean

A reward being listed here does not automatically mean it can be earned today. Some collections are retired, some depend on an enabled event, and some systems have been prepared but are not currently available. I keep the historical entries because they explain older collections, but their pages distinguish them from the current game.

For species and gameplay tables, the wiki records the values Sharkdle uses. A size category, a discovery year or an RNG rating is a game reference value; it should not be confused with a complete scientific species account or a fixed real-world rarity.

Looking for something specific? Use the search bar, open a topic in the sidebar, or browse all pages A–Z. Large reference tables have their own filters. The guide index keeps walkthroughs separate from the species reference pages.
